How hiring works

The same four steps for every role, from teaching positions to administration.

I.

Apply

Submit your resume and a short note on why you'd fit the role and our school community.

II.

Screening call

A 20-minute call with our HR team to talk through the role and answer your questions.

III.

Interview & demo

Teaching roles include a short demo lesson; other roles meet with the relevant department head.

IV.

Offer

References are checked and an offer follows, with relocation support where needed.
